# Lemon Emerald Rogue

## What is Lemon Emerald Rogue?

It's a (mostly just bugfix!) branch of Pokabbie's Emerald Rogue that I maintain to fix some of the more common bugs, and hopefully to provide support for an external calculator that I'm still developing.

## What is pokeemerald-expansion?

It's the engine that this game is based on. It's the engine that like, most modern romhacks are built on actually? Like think hacks like Dekzeh's Rhythm and Blues or Rain's Louis Sidney, or Terrra's Terrra Emerald which releases when? That's right, tomorrow.

## What is the ROM Hacking Hideout?

A Discord-based ROM hacking community that has many members who hack using the disassembly and decompilation projects for Pokémon. Quite a few contributors to the original feature branches by DizzyEgg were members of ROM Hacking Hideout. You can call it RHH for short! (It's also where I, Lemon, the developer of Lemon Emerald Rogue, basically source 100% of my bugfixes from. Because it turns out that people who are actually good at this are better at this than I am)
